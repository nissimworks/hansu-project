About us

The city has the money. It does not have the person who answers the phone.

Busan holds about KRW 1.57 trillion in startup funds and ranks among the world's top 70 emerging startup ecosystems. A founder here can find a grant, a desk and a class. What almost none can find is one experienced person who remembers last month's problem and picks up the phone.

Retired executives in this city have exactly that experience, and no structured way to use it. 한수 프로젝트 (Hansu Project) is the structure. We vet and train the senior executives, match each of them with a founder, and handle everything except the conversation.

한수 is a move in baduk. 한 수 배우다, to learn one move from someone who plays better, is an ordinary phrase in Korean. We took it literally.

The two sides sit on one board. One has played longer, and that is the only difference we recognise.

Who is who

한수 선배 경영인 for the retired executives. 한수 창업가 for the founders. 동문 for both sides once a cohort finishes. We do not use the words mentor and mentee as titles: one has played longer, and both are responsible for the game.

Who runs it

닛심워크 (Nissim Work), a consulting company in Busan, designs and operates the program. It is founded and run by Nofar Lupo, one person today. The founding circle has three seats for founding senior executives and two of them are still open, so the first full cohort follows the pilot. Applications and questions come to a person, not a form queue.

The program

Two pillars, one purpose

A person for the founder, and a tool for every founder in the city. The person is slow and deep. The tool is fast and free.

Pillar one

한수 선배 경영인, one to one

Retired executives, sixty and over, with fifteen or more years of operating responsibility. We vet them, train them, and match each one with one or two founders for six months.

  • Two ninety-minute sessions a month, in person or online
  • A review at month three: continue, change, or stop
  • No money in either direction, and no investment for twelve months after
  • Scheduling, space and notes are handled by one person, not a queue
Pillar two

Startup Support Navigator

A free bilingual tool that shows a founder which Busan and national programs they can actually apply for, what each one pays, and how to apply. No login, no data collected, no per-user cost.

  • 27 programs, filtered by age, stage, district and sector
  • Every card carries the date it was last checked
  • Foreign founders get the same answer, not a footnote

Questions

Short answers

Does it cost anything?

No. Nothing is charged to the founder or the senior executive, no equity is taken, and no money passes between the two of them. At the founding stage the running cost is paid by Nissim Work alone, and we will tell you exactly who funds the program before you commit.

Does it run in English?

Yes. Sessions run in Korean or English, and foreign founders in Busan are matched the same way as Korean founders. The navigator tool is bilingual.

Who qualifies as a senior executive?

Sixty or over, retired or semi-retired, with fifteen or more years of operating responsibility: a company you built, a division you ran, an institution you led. Sector matters less than judgment, and we ask for one area where you are genuinely strong and one where you are not.

What does the navigator show, and is it current?

Twenty-seven Busan and national programs, with amounts, eligibility, application windows and links. Every figure was checked in November 2026 against the institution's own page, and every card carries its last-checked date. Program dates change each year, so re-check any figure before you quote it.

Can I see the full proposal?

Yes. Write to nissimworks@gmail.com and tell us who you are and what you need. We send the one-pager first, then the full blueprint, the senior executive pack or the founder pack.
