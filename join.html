Two ways to the table

테이블로 오는 두 가지 길

Two doors, and you only need one. Below each door are the steps for that side, and a person to write to. Korean or English, and we answer within two working days.

For founders

You are building a company

Twenty to forty-five, pre-seed to Series A, registered in Busan or about to be. You have a real decision in front of you in the next three months, and you will show up twice a month. Korean or foreign, both welcome.

Send three things:

  1. You and the company: name, company, industry, stage, one line about what you do.
  2. The decision that is blocking you, and the door you want opened.
  3. A time this week for a twenty-minute call.

For senior executives

You have run something

Sixty or over, fifteen or more years of operating responsibility, retired or semi-retired, with four to six hours a month. The founding circle has three seats and two are open.

Send three things:

  1. You: name, last role and industry.
  2. Two areas where you are strongest, and one big decision from the last five years.
  3. The time you can give each month, and two people we can speak to, one of whom you led.

Before you decide

Three things we will tell you first

  • Who else is in the founding circle, and who is still missing. Two seats are open.
  • Who pays for the program. At the founding stage it is Nissim Work itself.
  • What happens at month three if the match is not working, and that either side can end it without explaining why.
A note on the buttons. Each one opens your own mail app with the questions already written, so nothing is sent until you send it. If your phone or computer has no mail app set up, write to nissimworks@gmail.com directly.
